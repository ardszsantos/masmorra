# Nerd kit

The character owns dash, book bonk, and super set square independently of the eight upgrade slots. Selecting Nerd selects the character; the separate information button opens a single-screen guide. All three moves and the passive are visible together, using looping illustrations with the existing Nerd sprite, animated input traces, attack effects, and short Portuguese captions. The information screen does not describe upgrades or inventory.

Two separately released strokes resolve within the existing one-second queue. Dash retains its existing direction tolerance, movement, damage, stamina cost, and cooldown. Turns beyond the dash tolerance and below 130 degrees throw the set square. Reversals from 130 to 180 degrees activate the book. Both attacks aim along the second stroke; the first stroke primes the combination. There are no unrecognized angle gaps. No unavailable ability falls back to a different action. Drawing continues to steer movement.

Knowledge fills only during active encounters: one stack per 12 seconds while moving, or one per 2 seconds after standing still for 1 second. The faster rate replaces the passive rate. Three completed stacks is the cap. Movement, attacks, damage, room transitions, and focus loss end reading; existing knowledge persists between rooms. Bonk consumes completed stacks and preserves partial progress. The hand socket displays an open book during reading; the HUD displays three lamps and progress toward the next one.

Bonk costs 20 stamina and has a 0.8-second cooldown. The Nerd grips the book with both hands, prepares for 0.12 seconds, jumps for 0.34 seconds, and recovers for 0.18 seconds. The book stays connected to the hands throughout the swing. Only ground contact consumes knowledge and spawns damage: a 55-damage smash within 48 pixels of the contact point, followed by a traveling 22-damage wavefront. Interrupting the jump restores the pose without producing an impact. Each target is hit once per cast. Zero stacks gives 150-pixel wave reach and a 55-degree cone; each consumed stack adds 65 pixels and 15 degrees. Walls block damage. These values are Inspector properties of the ability and effect. The tutorial uses the same pose choreography.

The set square costs 15 stamina and steers through a half-ellipse before returning to the moving player. Its nominal reach is 360 pixels, with 650-pixel/second outward speed and 900-pixel/second return speed. It acquires living, unobstructed enemies within 280 pixels, favoring the sector indicated by the second stroke. Tracking fades at the turnaround so the flight retains its curve. It deals 28 damage once per target on each leg. Its small physical collision shape slides along walls without despawning or immediately switching to return. When the way home is blocked, it retraces the actual flight path and follows the player's recorded movement. Walls still block damage. Flight does not block dash or bonk. Taking damage does not prematurely recover the projectile. Room transitions, death, removal, and focus loss clean up owned effects.

The upgrade controller enforces eight unique upgrades; duplicate rewards still level up an equipped upgrade. Full inventories exclude unequippable new upgrades from exit offers.

Retired slash, spin, and circle recognition are preserved under `archive/legacy_abilities/`. Existing controls and gameplay tests still describe parts of the retired kit and need migration when testing is authorized. No automated checks, editor runs, gameplay runs, or visual checks were performed during this implementation.
